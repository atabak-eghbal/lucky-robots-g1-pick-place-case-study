These notes are not generic dictionary definitions. Each note explains what the concept means, why it matters in robotics, and how it appears in this specific G1 pick-and-place baseline.

Simulation and Runtime

MuJoCo

Physics simulator for the Unitree G1, tables, cameras, and cylinder.

Simulation environment and runtime loop.

Freejoint

Lets the cylinder move freely in 3D with position and orientation updates.

Simulation joints and object state.

Decimation

Controls how many physics steps happen per high-level control update.

Timing, stability, and policy expectations.

PD Control

Position/velocity feedback that turns policy outputs into joint motion.

Actuation and target tracking.

Learned Policy Runtime

ONNX Runtime

Runs the pre-trained walker and reacher neural-network policies.

Model execution and inference pipeline.

Walker Policy

Learned locomotion controller that outputs body and joint commands.

Navigation and gait stability.

Reacher Policy

Learned controller that drives the right arm toward a target.

Arm overlay and manipulation.

Internal Normalization

Observation scaling handled inside the model instead of outside.

Model assumptions and preprocessing.
