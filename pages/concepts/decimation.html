Control Decimation

Decimation means the physics simulator steps multiple times for every high-level control update.

What it means

MuJoCo physics may run at 200 Hz while the controller updates at a slower rate. With decimation = 4, one control action is held across four physics steps.

Why it matters

Learned policies expect a certain update cadence. Decimation stabilizes runtime, reduces compute load, and aligns physics with policy expectations.

How it appears in this repo

  • Physics runs at 200 Hz with a timestep of 0.005 s.
  • Control updates run at 50 Hz with decimation = 4.
  • The grasp backend may tick during physics substeps.

Design implications

  • Timing changes affect every timeout and FSM threshold.
  • Grasp and vision updates must align with the physics tick.
  • Policy expectations should be validated after any timing change.

Common failure modes or misunderstandings

  • Updating grasp only at control rate.
  • Misinterpreting ticks as seconds.
  • Changing decimation without retuning timeouts.
