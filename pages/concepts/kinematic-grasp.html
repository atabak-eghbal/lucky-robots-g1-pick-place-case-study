Kinematic Grasp

A kinematic grasp directly attaches the object to the hand in simulation instead of relying on physical contact.

What it means

A kinematic grasp is a simulation shortcut. It checks if the palm is close and the grip is closed, then updates the object pose to follow the palm. Collisions may be disabled while attached.

Why it matters

It makes task sequencing testable without solving real grasp physics. This enables early-stage validation of the FSM and policy integration.

How it appears in this repo

  • Attachment triggers when the palm is close and grip is closed.
  • The object pose is overwritten to follow the palm during carry.
  • Release restores collisions and returns control to physics.

Design implications

  • Great for sequencing validation, but not a real grasp solution.
  • Attach thresholds must be tuned to avoid false positives.
  • Release logic must restore normal physics behavior.

Common failure modes or misunderstandings

  • Claiming physical grasping is solved.
  • Setting attach threshold too large.
  • Hiding real contact instability.
  • Failing to release collision state.
