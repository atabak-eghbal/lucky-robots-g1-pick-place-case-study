MuJoCo

MuJoCo is the physics simulator used to run the Unitree G1 robot, tables, cameras, and red cylinder.

What it means

  • Simulates rigid-body dynamics, contacts, joints, actuators, and cameras.
  • XML files define the robot and scene configuration.
  • It is not the real world—everything is deterministic and idealized.

Why it matters

MuJoCo enables controlled experiments that would be expensive or unsafe on real hardware. It is critical for rapid iteration, but it does not guarantee sim-to-real transfer.

How it appears in this repo

  • Robot and scene definitions live in g1.xml and scene.xml.
  • The physics timestep is 0.005 seconds (200 Hz) for this baseline.
  • The simulator drives cameras, contacts, and policy observations.

Design implications

  • Deterministic simulation makes debugging repeatable.
  • Timing changes (timestep/decimation) affect controller stability.
  • Simulation shortcuts must be clearly labeled as non-realistic.

Common failure modes or misunderstandings

  • Assuming simulation success implies real-world success.
  • Misconfigured XML models that alter joint ordering or geometry.
  • Timestep mismatches that destabilize learned policies.
  • Ignoring contact or visual artifacts specific to the simulator.
