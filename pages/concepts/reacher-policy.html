Reacher Policy

The reacher policy is the learned controller that moves the right arm toward a target.

What it means

right_reacher.onnx maps arm observations and a target pose to arm actions. It is used as an overlay on top of walker outputs to keep the arm moving toward the task target.

Why it matters

The reacher keeps the arm in-distribution while the humanoid walks. Without it, the walker policy sees unexpected arm states and becomes unstable.

How it appears in this repo

  • It expects pelvis-frame target coordinates.
  • It runs unconditionally to keep the arm in-distribution.
  • It showed a practical accuracy floor around 12 cm.

Design implications

  • Targets must be converted into the pelvis frame every tick.
  • Accuracy limits shape grasp thresholds and timeouts.
  • Turning it off destabilizes walking because arm joints drift.

Common failure modes or misunderstandings

  • Expecting perfect convergence.
  • Sending target in the wrong frame.
  • Turning it off and destabilizing walking.
  • Over-tight thresholds.
