Evidence & Provenance

The implementation is not just code; it's a history of decisions. Every threshold in fsm_core.py corresponds to a measurement in the Development Log.

Staff Engineer Note

The modularity of grasp.py and fsm_core.py was critical for resolving the "Release NaN" bug. Because the collision logic was isolated, we could tune the physical restoration delay without re-verifying the high-level FSM transitions.

Step 24–26 continues this pattern: physical-grasp infrastructure is implemented and test-validated, but the physically successful lift remains an active tuning target. The kinematic/FSM path remains the reliable teacher baseline while contact-aware caging is iterated as a research branch.
