Limitations

  • The baseline is a debuggable modular controller, not a production-ready robotics stack.
  • Kinematic grasping is a simulation shortcut that hides contact dynamics and finger forces.
  • The Visual Oracle is an architectural design, not an implemented module in this submission.
  • Target-side alignment relies on empirical threshold tuning, not closed-loop pose feedback.
  • Post-DONE on-table validation is not implemented; placement success was confirmed by visual inspection of the simulation.
  • The wrist camera is not exploited in the current baseline.
  • Sim-to-real transfer has not been attempted and will require additional validation.

Next Steps

  • Implement the Visual Oracle: RGB segmentation, depth back-projection, EMA smoothing, and freeze logic.
  • Add post-DONE proximity checks to programmatically confirm on-table placement.
  • Add contact-aware grasping with slip detection and physical release validation.
  • Refine target-side navigation with closed-loop yaw and distance feedback.
  • Apply domain randomization and sensor noise for robustness evaluation.
  • Expand dashboards for timing, failure recovery, and pose drift metrics.

Research-informed future work

The next step is not to replace the FSM immediately with a black-box policy. The safer path is to upgrade perception and validation first, then compare the explicit FSM baseline against learned-policy alternatives.

SAM 2 for object masks

SAM 2 is a promptable segmentation model for images and videos. In this repo, it would be most useful as a future source-object mask generator for head or wrist camera frames.

Future / proposed

FoundationPose for 6D pose

FoundationPose is relevant because the red cylinder has a simple known geometry. A future perception stack could combine segmentation with 6D pose tracking to estimate a world-frame grasp target instead of reading MuJoCo ground truth.

Future / proposed

OpenVLA / openpi as learned-policy comparators

Open VLA-style policies are useful future comparison points, but they should be evaluated against this deterministic baseline rather than substituted blindly.

Future / proposed

Humanoid foundation-model context

Systems such as GR00T and Helix 02 show where whole-body humanoid control is moving: tighter integration between perception, locomotion, manipulation, and language-conditioned behaviour. They are strategic context, not evidence for this implementation.
