Honest Engineering: Current Limitations

  • Kinematic Grasping: The cylinder is teleported to the palm rather than grasped via physical finger contact. Moving to contact-rich grasping is the most significant next step for physical realism.
  • Physical dexterous grasp remains unsolved: The kinematic backend completes the task as a simulation shortcut, while the contact-aware/table-assisted caging branch is more physically honest but can still slip during lift. The object is small and easy to push, the fingers are position-controlled, and there is no learned tactile grasp policy yet.
  • Ground-Truth Dependency: While navigation and transport are autonomous, they rely on knowing the object's simulated pose. Implementing the Visual Oracle design is required for full camera-only autonomy.
  • Environment Variance: The current thresholds are tuned for a specific table-block arrangement. Generalizing to different heights and lighting conditions remains future work.

The Product Roadmap

1. Visual Integration

Execute the Visual Oracle perception design using RGB-D camera streams.

2. Physical Grasping

Continue tuning contact-aware caging with better wrist alignment, slower grip schedules, contact confirmation before lift, friction tuning, and slip prevention.

3. End-to-End Learning

Collect data from the FSM baseline to train a residual RL policy for robust manipulation.

Scripted Teacher Needs Grasp Assurance

Step 23A added a scripted keyboard teacher and proved that automated keyboard-like control can be recorded into the VLA pipeline. However, the first open-loop macro did not pick up the cylinder. The next step is to add attachment checks, retry logic, and task-success metrics before using scripted demos as teacher data.
