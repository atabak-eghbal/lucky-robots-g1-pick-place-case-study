Honest Engineering: Current Limitations

  • Kinematic Grasping: The cylinder is teleported to the palm rather than grasped via physical finger contact. Moving to contact-rich grasping is the most significant next step for physical realism.
  • Ground-Truth Dependency: While navigation and transport are autonomous, they rely on knowing the object's simulated pose. Implementing the Visual Oracle design is required for full camera-only autonomy.
  • Environment Variance: The current thresholds are tuned for a specific table-block arrangement. Generalizing to different heights and lighting conditions remains future work.

The Product Roadmap

1. Visual Integration

Execute the Visual Oracle perception design using RGB-D camera streams.

2. Physical Grasping

Replace kinematic attachment with a force-control finger backend.

3. End-to-End Learning

Collect data from the FSM baseline to train a residual RL policy for robust manipulation.

Hybrid Replay Before OpenVLA

The Step 15 arm-only replay produced a useful negative result: the replay harness worked, but 7D palm deltas alone could not reproduce the full task because locomotion was missing. Mean palm error was 0.264 m and ever_attached remained False.

The next step is Step 16: record and replay walker commands alongside arm actions, and compare teacher-command replay against hybrid 7D replay before attempting OpenVLA shadow inference.
