Full-Task Demonstration

This video demonstrates the G1 humanoid autonomously performing the pick-and-place task. The sequence includes the initial stabilization, approach, grasp, 180-degree turn, transport, and stable release on the target table.

Key Highlights in the Clip

  • 0:03 - 0:08: Approach and Descend. Notice the precision of the right arm as it hovers above the cylinder.
  • 0:11: Kinematic Attachment. The cylinder is "snapped" into the palm offset.
  • 0:15 - 0:35: Transport Phase. The robot carries the object while performing a complex 180-degree turn.
  • 0:45: The "Grace Period" Release. Observe the stable placement on the blue table, enabled by the 15-tick collision delay.
