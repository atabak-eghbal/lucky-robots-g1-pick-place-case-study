Architectural Vision: The Perception Extension

Implementation Status: Designed architectural target.

The baseline uses ground-truth pose lookup to establish a clean locomotion and manipulation control loop. The Visual Oracle is designed to replace that lookup with a camera-based localization pipeline.

Deterministic Perception

The estimator is designed to be lightweight and deterministic. By using depth back-projection and EMA smoothing, we recover object poses without the latency or stochasticity of a learned detector.

The Freeze Strategy: A key part of the design is freezing the estimate during the final descent. This prevents occlusion jitter from the wrist camera destabilizing the grasp—a common pitfall in perception-driven manipulation.
